# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss technical decisions on their merits.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Keep private information private.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation,
publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. Serious incidents
may be reported privately through the repository's security reporting channel.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
